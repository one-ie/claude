## FAQ

**Are all 27 roles included from day one?** Yes, on Agency and Scale plans. Starter includes the 3 core groups (Marketing, Sales, Service) but with simplified role sets.

**Can I add custom roles?** On Scale: yes. You define the role in markdown, assign skills and tool access, publish. It joins the team.

**How does escalation work?** Each role has an `escalate:` field in its markdown. When confidence drops below the threshold or the query matches a keyword, the signal routes to the next role. The client manager gets notified if it reaches the top of the chain.

**Can the manager review gate block a response?** Yes. The agent pauses and notifies the configured reviewer. The reviewer approves, edits, or rejects from the inbox. Response sends on approval.

**Do roles share memory?** Within a client workspace, roles can read shared context (e.g., the CRM actor record). Within each role, task-specific memory is isolated.

**What's the cost vs. hires?** 27 hires at average UK salary = £1.2M–£2M annually. ONE Agency plan: £5,000/mo = £60k/year. You earn the £1.14M–£1.94M delta — or pass it to your client as a saving.

**Is there a trial?** Yes. 14-day full-access trial on Agency. No credit card on day one.
