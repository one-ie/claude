The window to be the agency that *owns* the AI layer, rather than the agency that buys it from a vendor, is not ten years wide. It is narrow.

ONE is that layer. You white-label it. You mark up the credits. You set the price. Your clients see your brand on the OAuth screen, your name on the invoice, your voice in every message the platform sends. They never learn ours.
