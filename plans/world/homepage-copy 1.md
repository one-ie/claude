## Appendix B — Tone guide

- Short sentences. Subjects carry weight.
- Numbers in **bold**, verbs in plain weight.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- Active voice, present tense (*"a wallet exists"*, not *"a wallet will be created"*).
- **Outcome before mechanism.** "Get paid in three seconds" beats "settle on-chain via sponsored Sui transactions". The mechanism belongs in deep docs.
- Concrete > clever. **5 seconds** beats *"fast"*; **0.5%** beats *"low fees"*; *"on-chain escrow"* beats *"trustless settlement"*.
- When in doubt, cut a sentence rather than soften one.

## Appendix E — Open decisions (flag before ship)

These need owner sign-off before this copy goes live.

1. **0.5% vs 2% marketplace fee.** Personas.md says "50bps protocol fee"; revenue.md says "2%". I copied **0.5%** here because that's what fits the H3 Maker promise ("paid for craft, not exposure" — 2% reads more like a real platform fee). Reconcile before ship.
2. **"Agents that earn while you sleep" claim.** Legal/copy review for jurisdictions that treat "earn" as a regulated promise.
3. **Live numbers in Section 10.** Need a server-side endpoint that returns `{ agents_live, gmv_24h, uptime, wallets_today }`. Until that exists, dim the line or omit the section.
4. **Section 9 grid order.** Currently founder → maker → buyer → chairman. A/B test order; H4 Buyer might convert better as the *first* card since it's the broadest fit.
5. **The "we can't take your money" claim (Section 8).** Strong claim. Verify nothing in the architecture lets us take user funds via a back-door (e.g., upgrade authority on a Move object, oracle override). If there's any mechanism, soften the claim.
