## Appendix B — Tone guide

- Short sentences. Subjects carry weight.
- Numbers in **bold**, verbs in plain weight.
- No exclamation marks. No emoji.
- Active voice, present tense. *"A wallet exists"* not *"A wallet will be created."*
- Outcome before mechanism. **5 seconds** beats *"fast"*; **0.5%** beats *"low fees"*; *"on-chain"* beats *"trustless settlement"*.
- When in doubt, cut a sentence rather than soften one.
- Five-word sentences are a feature, not a poverty.

## Appendix E — Open decisions (flag before ship)

1. **0.5% vs 2% marketplace fee.** [`personas.md`](one.ie/one/personas.md) and [`world.md`](world.md) say "50bps protocol fee"; [`one.ie/one/revenue.md`](one.ie/one/revenue.md) says "2%". This copy locks **0.5%** because that's what closes the H3 Maker pitch. Reconcile before ship.
2. **"Make your AI agent earn" — legal review.** Some jurisdictions treat *"earn"* as a regulated promise. Verify this language is OK or soften to *"AI agents that get paid for what they do."*
3. **Live status numbers (Section 6 / footer).** Need a server-side endpoint returning `{ agents_live, gmv_24h, uptime, wallets_today }`. Until that exists, omit the live strip.
4. **"Connect your agent" CTA destination.** Need a `/connect` flow that accepts existing agent specs (LangChain JSON, OpenAI Assistants API, MCP server URL, custom). This is the highest-leverage product surface for the "bring your agent" pitch — confirm scope.
5. **Branded-AI section claim.** *"This is the part nobody else has the architecture to credibly offer"* — defensible if no competitor has groups + pheromone routing + on-chain settlement composed. Spot-check before ship.
